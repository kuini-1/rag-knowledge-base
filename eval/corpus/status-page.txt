LARKSPUR STATUS PAGE - HOW IT WORKS

The status page at status.larkspur.example shows the current health of every region and service.

Components are shown in one of four states: Operational, Degraded performance, Partial outage, and Major outage. A component is marked Degraded when its error rate is above 1% or its median latency is more than twice the usual value for five minutes.

Incidents are posted within 10 minutes of being confirmed by the on-call engineer. Updates follow at least every 30 minutes until the incident is resolved. A written incident report is published within five business days for any incident that lasted longer than 30 minutes or affected more than one region.

You can subscribe to updates by email, SMS, RSS, or webhook. Webhook subscribers receive a JSON payload for every incident update, signed with an HMAC-SHA256 signature in the X-Larkspur-Signature header.

Scheduled maintenance is announced on the status page at least 72 hours in advance. Maintenance that may cause downtime is scheduled between 01:00 and 05:00 local time for the affected region.

The status page is hosted on a separate provider so that it stays available during a Larkspur outage.
