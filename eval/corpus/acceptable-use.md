# Acceptable Use Policy

You are responsible for everything that runs in your organization, including what your users and customers store or send through it.

## Prohibited activities

You may not use Larkspur Cloud to:

- store or distribute malware, or host phishing pages;
- mine cryptocurrency, including through compute attached to storage events;
- send unsolicited bulk email or host the infrastructure for it;
- store content that sexually exploits minors, which we report to the relevant authorities;
- scan, probe, or attack systems you do not own or have permission to test.

## Public buckets

Public buckets are allowed for websites, downloads, and open datasets. A public bucket that serves more than 50 TB of egress in a month is reviewed to confirm it is not being used as a general-purpose CDN for a third party.

## Enforcement

For most violations we email the organization Owners and give them 72 hours to fix the problem. We may suspend access immediately, without notice, when an activity harms other customers or the platform, or when the law requires it.

## Reporting abuse

Report abuse to abuse@larkspur.example with the bucket name or URL. We acknowledge abuse reports within 24 hours.
