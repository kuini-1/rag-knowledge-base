# Billing FAQ

## When am I charged?

Invoices are issued on the first day of each month for the previous month's usage and plan fee. Payment is due within 15 days of the invoice date.

## Which payment methods do you accept?

Credit and debit cards on all paid plans. Business and Enterprise customers can also pay by bank transfer. We do not accept cryptocurrency.

## Which currencies can I pay in?

You can be invoiced in US dollars, euros, or Thai baht. The currency is chosen when you add a payment method and can be changed once per year by contacting support.

## Do you charge VAT or sales tax?

Yes, where required. EU customers with a valid VAT number are invoiced under the reverse-charge mechanism. Customers in Thailand are charged 7% VAT unless they provide a withholding tax certificate.

## Can I get a refund?

Plan fees paid annually can be refunded in full within 14 days of purchase if the account has used less than 10 GB of egress. After that, annual fees are not refundable, but you keep access until the end of the paid period. Monthly fees are not refunded.

## What happens if a payment fails?

We retry the card three times over seven days and email the billing contact each time. If the invoice is still unpaid 30 days after its due date, the organization is suspended: data stays intact, but API requests fail until the balance is paid.

## How do I cancel?

Owners can cancel from the console under Settings, then Billing. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing period. After that, the organization moves to Starter, and data above the Starter limits must be deleted within 30 days or it is removed.
