# Support

Your plan determines how fast we respond and how you can reach us.

## Support tiers

| Tier | Plans | Channels | Hours |
|---|---|---|---|
| Community | Starter | Forum | Best effort |
| Standard | Team | Email, tickets | Business hours, Monday to Friday |
| Priority | Business | Email, tickets, chat | 24/7 for P1 and P2, business hours otherwise |
| Premier | Enterprise | All channels plus phone and a shared Slack channel | 24/7 |

Business hours are 9:00 to 18:00 in the customer's chosen support region: Americas, Europe, or Asia-Pacific.

## Severity levels

- P1: production is down or data is at risk.
- P2: production is degraded or a major feature is unavailable.
- P3: a minor feature is broken or there is a workaround.
- P4: questions and feature requests.

## First response times

| Severity | Standard | Priority | Premier |
|---|---|---|---|
| P1 | 8 hours | 1 hour | 15 minutes |
| P2 | 1 business day | 4 hours | 1 hour |
| P3 | 2 business days | 1 business day | 4 hours |
| P4 | 3 business days | 2 business days | 1 business day |

## Languages

Support is available in English on every tier. Priority and Premier also cover Japanese and Thai during Asia-Pacific business hours.

## Escalation

If a ticket is not progressing, reply with the word ESCALATE and a support manager will review it within one business day. Premier customers can escalate directly through their technical account manager.
